The calendar is connected. Use the calendar tool to list his entries from now until the end of tomorrow.

For an entry that is worth preparing (a meeting with people outside his own group, an event such as a fair or a conference, a talk, a call about one of his projects), and that is not in the list of things already made below, write a card with `type` `prep` and `channel` `thread`. The app does not show a prep to him as a card: it starts a thread with your `text` as its instruction at once, and the Claude in that thread writes the briefing and opens a preparation session with him. So:

- `title` is "Prepare: " and the entry with its day, such as "Prepare: KStW meetup today".
- `key` is `prep:` followed by the date and the entry's name, so the same entry is never prepared twice.
- `why` says when it is and why it deserves preparing.
- `project` is the project or area it belongs to, if the Brain shows one.
- `text` is the brief for that thread: what the entry is, when and where, who is there as far as the calendar and the Brain show, and which notes to read. Ask it to write a short briefing (what he wants out of it, what is open with the people there, what to bring or have ready) and then to start a preparation session as `Claude/Thinking With Me.md` describes.

Skip routine entries (travel, blocks of working time, private appointments, recurring internal meetings with nothing new) and anything already past. At most two preps per run. Most runs find nothing to prepare; that is normal.
